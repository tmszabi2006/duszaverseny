# Új bányaképek – mélységek

Hat új jelenet a beépített képgenerálóval. A helyi képatlasz: `client/public/assets/mine-atlas-depths.webp`. A teljes választék 15 kép; a pályaazonosító alapján stabilan váltakoznak.

## Prompt

Create one landscape 3:2 game environment texture atlas with exactly 3 columns and 2 rows of six equal square illustrations, no margins, borders, text, UI or watermarks. Premium detailed painterly realistic 3D winter mining adventure art, amber lanterns, stone, aged timber and machinery. Every panel independent with a strong centered subject safe for wide card crops. Top left: snowy cliffside cable-car ore station over a mountain ravine at blue hour. Top middle: underground emerald vein with green crystals, copper pipes and a minecart. Top right: abandoned snowy railway turntable inside a round timber engine shed, golden light. Bottom left: immense ancient stone mining chamber with carved pillars and treasure ore, warm dust shafts. Bottom middle: icy underground waterfall beside a wooden stairway, cold silver blue light and amber lamps. Bottom right: deep coal mine with black rock, red industrial lift machinery and orange safety lamps. All six have distinct composition and palettes, consistent mining game style, believable detailed materials. No people, no labels, no grid borders.

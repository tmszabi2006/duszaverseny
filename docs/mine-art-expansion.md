# További bányaképek

A beépített képgenerálóval készült hat új jelenet, egy 3 × 2 képatlaszban. A korábbi hárommal együtt kilenc kártyaillusztráció érhető el. Az atlasz WebP formátumban, helyben töltődik be: `client/public/assets/mine-atlas-expansion.webp`.

## Generálási prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production game environment texture atlas, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, precisely 3 columns by 2 rows of SIX equal square illustrations with NO gaps, borders, labels or text. Each square is an independent richly detailed cinematic mining location, painterly realistic 3D game art, warm amber lanterns and cool winter light, timber, stone and metal. Top left: snowy mountain mine railway exterior with small mining locomotive and pine forest. Top middle: enormous underground amethyst geode cavern with wooden walkway and violet crystals. Top right: abandoned vertical mining shaft with lift cage, winding machinery and warm lanterns. Bottom left: underground flooded silver mine with reflective blue lake, little timber jetty and ore cart. Bottom middle: deep volcanic copper mine with red orange glowing fissures, heavy stone and copper ore, safe wooden bridge. Bottom right: winter mining base camp with snow covered cabins, ore sacks, pickaxe and golden lamps under a starry night. Compose each square with main subject near its center so it works when cropped as a wide website card. Visually distinct silhouettes and color moods, consistent premium adventure game art. No people, no text, no watermarks, no UI. Edge to edge exact six square panel atlas.
